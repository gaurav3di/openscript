# math/independent-extreme-ages

The three-position window at bar 2 has a missing high but lows [2,1,2].
The lowest low is one position old, giving 100*(2-1)/2 = 50. At bar 3 all
highs are known and the latest is highest, giving 100. Its low window [1,2,3]
has the oldest minimum, giving zero. A common guard loses the known low age.
