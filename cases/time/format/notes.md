# time/format

`date.format` read back through a comparison and a length, because a plot
carries a number.

## What it pins

`stdlib.md` 12.3: the placeholders are filled and the dashes are copied, so
`yyyy-MM-dd` is ten characters on every bar. The exact text of a formatted time
is asserted by `unit` tests in both engines; this case holds that the call runs
and answers a string in both.
