# draw/label

A label with its defaults and one with every argument written.

## What it pins

`stdlib.md` 14.4: a label is one anchor and a plate of text; with nothing
written its plate has no colour, its text is `white`, it is centred and it has
no tooltip.

## Reference

Worked out by hand by the author of this case from the script's own arguments,
the defaults `stdlib.md` section 14.4 prints beside each creation call, and the
channel values of `spec/colours.json`, under this repository's licence.
