# table/fixed-shape

A grid whose row count is an input, written at its last row.

## What it pins

`compiled-program.md` 2.8 and 2.3: the declaration's rows come from the input's
value, settled before bar 0, so row 2 of three is inside the grid.

## Reference

Worked out by hand by the author of this case from the script's own arguments,
the defaults `stdlib.md` section 14.3 prints beside `cell`, and the
channel values of `spec/colours.json`, under this repository's licence.
