# table/cell-content

Four cells, two of them computed from the bar.

## What it pins

`stdlib.md` 14.3 and `conformance.md` section 4: each cell is the grid's title,
its row and column from zero, and the text and colours the last bar wrote;
`textColor` and `bgColor` default to none and `align` to `"left"`.

## Reference

Worked out by hand by the author of this case from the script's own arguments,
the defaults `stdlib.md` section 14.3 prints beside `cell`, and the
channel values of `spec/colours.json`, under this repository's licence.
